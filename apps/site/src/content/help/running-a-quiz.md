## Starting a question

When a quiz slide is on the screen, the answers are hidden and phones show *Get ready*. Press **Start the timer** and everyone sees the answers at the same moment.

## Points

A correct answer is worth up to **1,000 points**: the full thousand for an instant answer, falling to 500 at the buzzer. A wrong answer, or no answer, scores nothing. The clock runs on our server, not on the phones, so a slow phone is never cheated of time, and an answer sent just before the buzzer still counts.

## Revealing the answer

The answer is revealed when the timer runs out, or as soon as everyone in the room has answered, whichever comes first. You can also press **Reveal now**. Each phone then shows whether it was right, the points it scored and its place in the room.

## The leaderboard

After the reveal, press **Show leaderboard** to put the top ten on the screen. Phones show the top five and highlight their own name.

## Running a question again

**Run again** clears the answers on that slide, so you can repeat it. Scores from other questions are kept.
