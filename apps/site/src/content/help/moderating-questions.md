## Approval before the screen

In the editor, tick **Approve each question before it appears on the screen** on a Q&A slide. New questions then wait for you. The person who sent one sees *Waiting for approval* on their phone.

## Where to moderate

While presenting, press **Questions** in the control bar to open the list beside your slides. If you are using your [phone as a remote](/help/phone-remote), the questions are there too.

For each question you can:

- **Show** a waiting question on the screen,
- mark it **Answered** once it has been dealt with, which moves it down,
- **Hide** it, which takes it off the screen and out of the results.

## The word filter

Offensive words are refused before they reach anyone, in questions, word clouds and nicknames. The person sending sees a polite note and can try again.

## Upvotes

People can upvote questions other than their own, once each. The screen shows the most upvoted first, so the question most of the room wants answered is at the top.
