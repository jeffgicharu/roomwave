The quickest way to learn Roomwave is to be your own audience. You need a laptop and a phone.

## 1. Open a presentation

Every new account starts with **A tour of Roomwave**, which has one of every kind of slide. Open it from your dashboard. If you would rather start fresh, press **New presentation** and choose a template or a blank page.

## 2. Press Present

The presentation opens full screen with a bar across the top: the address to visit and a six-digit code, and a QR code in the corner. Press **Q** at any time to show the code large enough for the back of the room.

## 3. Join from your phone

Point your phone's camera at the QR code and open the link, or go to the address and type the code. There is nothing to install and no account to make.

## 4. Move to a question and answer it

Press the right arrow on your laptop. Your phone follows to the same slide. Tap an answer and watch the bar rise on the big screen.

## 5. Look at the results

When you are done, press **End**. Every answer is saved: open **Results** on the presentation to see each session, download a spreadsheet, or on Pro, a PDF report.

That is the whole loop. Everything else is choosing better questions.
