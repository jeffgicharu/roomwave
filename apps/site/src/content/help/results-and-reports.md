## Every session is kept

Each time you present, the answers are saved as a separate session. Open a presentation's **Results** to see every session with its date, the number of people and the number of answers.

If you edit a presentation and present it again while its session is still running, the session picks up your changes and keeps the answers already given.

## Spreadsheet

**Spreadsheet (CSV)** downloads every slide's answers: each option with its count and share, each word with its count, each question with its upvotes. It opens in Excel and Google Sheets.

## PDF report (Pro)

**PDF report** draws every chart and the quiz leaderboard into a report you can share with your team. **Email it to me** sends it to the email on your account, with the main numbers in the message.

## Deleting results

When a session has ended you can delete its results from the session's page. The presentation itself stays.
